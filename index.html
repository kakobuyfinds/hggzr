Before ordering through CSSBuy

  1. Open the HipobuyFind product page and check the original seller link, selected size, color and price.
  2. If you choose to order through CSSBuy, check whether it accepts the original seller URL and confirm the item and domestic delivery charges.
  3. If warehouse QC photos are available, compare them with the variant you ordered. Check measurements, stitching, print placement and any visible damage before approving shipment.
  4. Estimate the total using the item price, domestic delivery, any service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the links on this spreadsheet go?

The two main buttons open HipobuyFind. Each product link opens its corresponding HipobuyFind listing, where you can check for the original seller link. This is an independent guide to browsing the catalog.

What does the approximate USD price cover?

It reflects the listed CNY item price converted to USD for comparison. Use the actual exchange rate and the shipping and fee estimates shown when you order.

What should I look for in warehouse QC photos?

Start with the exact color, size and quantity you ordered. For shoes, look at both sides and the soles. For clothing, request measurements in centimeters if fit is uncertain. For bags, check the closures, straps and visible seams.

What if the original seller link has expired?

Search the item name on HipobuyFind and compare any replacement listing by photos, measurements, seller details and price before ordering.
